## Decisions already taken (comments in #46 and #47, 13–14/11/2026)
- **Identifier (David):** `patientId` is generated by the server (`P-0001`, `P-0002`…); `identifier` is the health card number and is unique, and it is what detects the duplicates; the national ID is a new optional field `nationalId`, because not every patient has one. Ignacio did not answer in #47 and the minutes of Meeting 8 do not mention it, so David still has to record it in `DECISIONS.md`.
- **Validation (David):** required are identifier, full name, date of birth and sex; the date of birth cannot be in the future; the e-mail, if given, must have the form `a@b.c`; the phone, if given, has 9 to 15 digits; the errors come together in a `ValidationException`. The client validates too (Ignacio and Marcos, NFR03).
- **Permission:** `MANAGE_PATIENTS`, given to the Admissions staff (Ignacio, #46; it is in C1 since the patch 1.2.2, PR #116). The same permission for registering, modifying, searching and reading. When C4 needs the physicians to read patients, it defines its own read permission (D-011).
- **Search:** `filters` is a text; a patient matches if it is contained, ignoring case, in the identifier or in the full name; an empty filter returns every patient.
- **Audit (D26):** every access and change is registered: `PATIENT_ACCESSED`, `PATIENT_REGISTERED` and `PATIENT_MODIFIED`. One entry per search and one per `getPatient`.
- **Seed data (David):** three example patients, invented: never real people.
- **Not in this version:** the discharge (`dischargePatient`) is in iteration 8 (D-024); no patient is deactivated here.
- **Tests:** they are Adam's (Testing): there is no step to write them here, only one to hand the branch over.
- `common` is already `1.4.0-SNAPSHOT` (D-036) and it is not changed.

## Questions and answers
Few questions, as Ignacio asked in Meeting 8 (E-19): all of them are answered, and the screens are not here (they are decided by Implementation).
- **Q1:** Module and packages. **Proposal:** a new module `server/c3-patients` (`artifactId` `server-c3-patients`, version `1.0.0-SNAPSHOT`, depends only on `common` and JUnit), package `es.uclm.esi.iso2.server.patients` with `persistence` and `service`; in `common`, the new packages `es.uclm.esi.iso2.common.patients.domain` and `es.uclm.esi.iso2.common.patients.contracts`, each with its `package-info.java`, as C2 did. Creating a module, packages and changing the poms needs the approval of Javier (rule 9). **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q2:** Names of the contract. **Proposal:** follow #47, the contract issue (D-008 and what Marcos said: the contracts count): `registerPatient(requester, patient)`, `modifyPatient(requester, patientId, patient)`, `getPatient(requester, patientId)` and `searchPatients(requester, filters)`, in an interface `IPatientService`. The design diagram has `updatePatient(requester, patient)`: Design has to follow #47. `requester` is the username as a `String`. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q3:** Data (`common.patients.domain`). **Proposal:** `Patient` (immutable): `patientId`, `identifier`, `nationalId` (optional), `fullName`, `dateOfBirth` as `LocalDate`, `sex`, `address`, `phone`, `email` and `active`; and the enum `Sex` with `FEMALE`, `MALE` and `UNSPECIFIED`, as in the design. `active` is always `true` in this version. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q4:** Duplicate patient. **Proposal:** `registerPatient` throws `DuplicatePatientException` (in `common.patients.contracts`, as in the design), which carries the existing patient, when the identifier already exists, comparing it ignoring case and spaces; nothing is saved. As the existing record is returned to the user, that is an access to patient data and it is registered as `PATIENT_ACCESSED`. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q5:** Rules and messages of the validation (all the errors together, in this order): "The patient is required"; "The identifier is required"; "The full name is required"; "The date of birth is required" or "The date of birth cannot be in the future"; "The sex is required"; "The e-mail must have the form name@domain.ext" (if given: no spaces, one `@` and a dot after it); "The phone must have 9 to 15 digits" (if given: the spaces and a leading `+` are ignored, and the rest must be only digits). The address and the national ID are free text. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q6:** Modification. **Proposal:** `modifyPatient` uses the `patientId` of the parameter; the identifier cannot be changed ("The identifier cannot be changed"), as the id in UC17; the national ID and the other fields can; a `patientId` that does not exist is "There is no patient with the id X". **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q7:** Audit names. **Proposal:** the resource is `patients` for a search (and for a denied registration) and `PATIENT:<patientId>` for the others; a denied access is `PATIENT_ACCESS_DENIED`, in every operation, as in UC17 and UC20. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q8:** Identifiers. **Proposal:** the server numbers the patients `P-0001`, `P-0002`… with a counter in the service, which continues after the seed data. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q9:** Repository. **Proposal:** its own `IPatientRepository` (`findById`, `findByIdentifier`, `search` and `save`, as in the design) and `InMemoryPatientRepository` (D-030), because C3 cannot depend on the generic repository of C2. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Q10:** Seed data. **Proposal:** `loadSeedData()` in the class only (not in the contract), called by `Main`, as in UC17 and UC20; it creates three invented patients only if there is none: all the data are made up, with health card numbers that do not exist. **A:** approved as proposed (Javier, 15/11/2026)
- **Note:** the diagram of Design still shows `UserAccount` as `requester`, `updatePatient`, a `Patient` without `nationalId` and `ValidationException` in the package of the contracts; the code follows #47 and the decisions above. Design has to reflect it (#114).
